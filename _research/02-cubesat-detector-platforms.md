The **DarkNESS CubeSat** program aims to search for two potential dark matter signatures from Low Earth Orbit. Through its mission, DarkNESS aims to demonstrate the first successful operation of skipper-CCDs in space.  
The satellite payload uses an array of **skipper-CCDs**, custom **LTA readout electronics**, and a **Ricor K508N cryocooler** to search for dark matter from Low Earth Orbit.
I am in charge of detector calibration, development of the in-flight analysis pipeline, and sensitivity predictions of the DarkNESS science reach.
