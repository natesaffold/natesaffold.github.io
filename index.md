    <p>
      I am a postdoctoral fellow at Fermilab, where I develop ultra-low-noise silicon imaging sensors for dark matter searches and astrophysics. My work centers on <strong>Skipper-CCD</strong> and <strong>SiSeRO</strong> technologies, sensors capable of resolving individual electrons, which I deploy in low-threshold dark matter searches such as <strong>SENSEI</strong> and in space-based missions like the <strong>DarkNESS CubeSat</strong>.
    </p> 
    <p>
      I earned my PhD in Physics from Columbia University in 2021, where I worked on the General Antiparticle Spectrometer (<strong>GAPS</strong>) experiment under Prof. Charles Hailey. Beyond research, I serve as co-chair of Fermilab's <strong>Saturday Morning Physics</strong> outreach program for high school students and co-organize the annual Chicago-area <strong>Dark Matter Day</strong> event.
